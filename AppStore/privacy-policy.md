# Privacy Policy for Sudoku Helper

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Sudoku Helper does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you.
- The puzzle you are playing and a setting that remembers you have seen the introduction are saved only on your own device, using the standard iOS storage. They never leave your device.
- The app does not use the internet.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
