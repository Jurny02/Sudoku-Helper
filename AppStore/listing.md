## Description
Sudoku Helper is for the puzzle you're already solving on paper or in another app.

Type in the starting numbers and the app shows you where each number can still go.

• Tap a square: only the numbers that fit its row, column and box stay active.
• Pick a number: the whole board is coloured for it. Yellow squares are the ones where it must go, green squares are where it can go, grey squares are blocked.
• Undo any move, and your game is saved automatically, so you can continue later.
• Watch It Solve shows the yellow-square method solving a puzzle step by step, with play, pause, speed control and a timer.
• A short How to Play guide and an example puzzle get you started.

No ads, no accounts and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
